Everything You Need

9 conversion-focused sections.
One theme.

Every section is a native Shopify 2.0 section with full settings, blocks, and presets. Drag, drop, and customize in the theme editor.

Core

VSL Hero

Full-width video section supporting YouTube, Vimeo, and MP4. Autoplay, background or inline layout, headline overlay, and CTA button. The centerpiece of every VSL funnel.

Quantity Breaks

Buy 1/3/6 with per-unit price drops. Highlights the best value tier. Savings badges and compare-at pricing. AJAX add-to-cart via Shopify's cart API.

Bundle Builder

Customers mix and match products into a custom bundle. Variant selectors, running total, discount badge, and single add-to-cart for the entire bundle.

Sticky Add-to-Cart

A fixed bar that slides in as visitors scroll past the main CTA. Shows product image, title, price, and an ATC button. Configurable scroll trigger threshold.

Dual Mode

Countdown Timer

Two modes: fixed end date for real deadlines, and evergreen that resets per visitor via localStorage. Urgency styling when time is low.

Social Proof Strip

Infinite-scrolling media logos, star rating display, and review/customer counts. Builds instant credibility with your "As Seen In" mentions.

Guarantee Block

Upload a custom badge or use the built-in shield icon. Fully customizable headline, rich text body, and colors. The risk reversal section every DR page needs.

FAQ Accordion

Clean accordion with smooth expand/collapse animations. Accessible markup with proper ARIA roles. Add unlimited Q&A pairs via the theme editor's block system.

Post-Purchase Upsell

One-click upsell shown on the thank-you page. Product image, discount badge, add-to-order CTA, and a soft decline link. Maximize AOV on every order.
